About us

A technology partner, grounded in the field.

Irradiant is an India-based technology consulting partnership. We bring deep domain expertise and a hands-on, partnership-led approach to managed hydration systems, AI-driven energy consulting and telecom infrastructure.

Who we are

Strategy that survives contact with the site.

Infrastructure projects succeed or fail on the ground. We work alongside our clients from the first site assessment to post-deployment validation, so that technical decisions are made with real operational data and are carried through to the finish.

Our work spans three domains that every modern organisation depends on. We approach each one as a long-term partner rather than a one-off advisor, grounded in expertise, transparency and lasting value.

Clean water

Treatment and alkaline water systems designed and deployed to institutional standards.

Reliable energy

AI and data-driven insight that makes energy systems more efficient and more resilient.

Strong connectivity

Telecom networks planned and designed to scale with the organisations that rely on them.
