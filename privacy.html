Irradiant Systems and Technologies ("Irradiant", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through irradiant.in, why we collect it, and the choices and rights you have.

1. Personal information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, for example when you submit an enquiry form, email us or message us on WhatsApp. This may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your organisation and the area of service you are interested in
  • The content of your message

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also automatically record basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and performance purposes.

2. Why we process your data

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, provide information about our services, prepare proposals, and maintain the security and performance of our website. We do not sell your personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

3. Data retention period

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to respond to you and manage any resulting business relationship, or as required by law. After that, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

4. International data transfers

Our website, email and related services may use cloud servers located outside India, including in Canada and the United States. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on reputable providers that apply appropriate safeguards to protect it.

5. Links to other websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or services, such as WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services and encourage you to review their policies.

6. Your rights under global frameworks

a. European Union (GDPR)

If you are in the EU or EEA, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

b. India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

You may request access to, correction of or erasure of your personal data, withdraw consent at any time, and nominate another person to exercise your rights. Grievances can be raised with our Grievance Officer at info@irradiant.in.

c. United States (CCPA and COPPA)

California residents may request to know what personal information we hold and to have it deleted. We do not sell personal information. Our website is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Legal disclosure

We may disclose personal information where required by law, regulation or legal process, or where necessary to protect our rights, our clients or the public.

8. Contact information

For any question about this policy or to exercise your rights, please contact us:

  • Email: info@irradiant.in
  • Website: irradiant.in

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the "last updated" date shown above.
